Privacy

Clear data practices for shared dog care.

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This policy explains what the PawTails iPhone app and this website do with your information, why, and how to remove it. Short version: no sign-up, no ads, no tracking, and you can erase everything from inside the app.

1. Who is responsible

PawTails is made by Gleb Tsurakov, an independent developer in Finland, who is the controller of your personal data. Contact: pawtails@proton.me. You can also use the Contact us link in the app.

2. What PawTails collects

  • Account identifier. A random ID created on your iPhone, linked to an anonymous Firebase sign-in. There is no email, password, phone number or real name.
  • Household data. Household name and photo, members' display names, photos and roles, and invites.
  • Pet care data. Pet name, photo and profile answers, routines, reminders, tasks and who completed them, and walk goals.
  • Walks you start. The GPS route (simplified to at most 600 points before syncing), distance, duration, and any mood, note and photo you add. Photos are stripped of location and camera metadata before upload.
  • Notifications. A push token, the app's language, and whether care alerts are on.
  • Usage statistics and crash reports, only if you tap Share. Screens and features used, app version, crash logs, and the account identifier above, so a crash can be matched to a report.
  • Messages you send us by email.

On your iPhone only, and never sent to us: your walk-suggestion home area and Motion & Fitness activity.

3. Why, and on what legal basis

  • To provide the app you asked for: syncing your household, recording and sharing walks, notifications and widgets. Legal basis: contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Usage statistics and crash reports to fix bugs and improve the app. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time in Preferences & account. Collection stops right away, and unsent reports are deleted.
  • Security: stopping abuse, checking that requests come from the genuine app (Apple App Attest), and answering your messages. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).

We never sell your data, show ads or track you across other apps and websites.

4. Who can see your data

  • Your household. Members you invite see the pet profile, routines, reminders, task history, member names and photos, and walks with their routes, notes and photos. Walks often start at home, so invite only people you trust. Owners can remove members at any time.
  • Service providers who process data for us under data-processing terms that require the same protection as this policy:
    • Google (Firebase): sign-in, database, server functions, push notifications, App Check, and, with your consent, Analytics and Crashlytics.
    • Apple: push notification delivery, App Attest and App Store purchases. We never see your payment details.
    • Cloudflare: hosts this website.

5. Where data is stored

The household database is in the European Union. Some processing happens in the United States: server functions for notifications and deletion run in Google's US region, and Analytics and Crashlytics use Google's US systems. These transfers are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, which Google and Cloudflare are certified under, and by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

6. Location

  • Location is optional. Routines, reminders and household sharing work without it.
  • Your route is recorded only during a walk you start, including while the screen is locked, and recording stops when you end the walk.
  • If you turn on walk suggestions and allow "Always" location, iOS tells PawTails when you leave your home area. PawTails then checks your location for up to 8 minutes to see whether you're walking. Nothing from that check is stored or sent.
  • You can turn location off at any time in iOS Settings.

7. How long we keep data

  • Household, pet and walk data: until you delete it in the app, or delete your account.
  • Usage statistics: up to 2 months. Crash reports: up to 90 days.
  • Emails: until your question is resolved, and at most 1 year.
  • Deleted data can remain in encrypted database backups for up to 14 weeks before it is overwritten. Backups are used only to recover from failures.

8. Deleting your data

In the app, go to Preferences & account → Delete my data / reset device account. This erases, on the server and on your iPhone:

  • dogs you own, with all their routines, tasks, walks and invites, for every member;
  • your walks in other households, your memberships and the invites you made;
  • your push tokens and your anonymous sign-in account.

You can also delete individual walks, remove a dog, or leave a household at any time. If you can't use the app, email us and we will delete your data.

9. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent. Email pawtails@proton.me and we will answer within one month. PawTails has no sign-in, so we may ask you for a detail from the app to confirm the data is yours. You can also complain to a data protection authority. In Finland that is the Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi).

10. This website

This website uses no cookies and no analytics. Cloudflare, which hosts it, and Google Fonts, which serves its fonts, receive your IP address to deliver the pages. Invite links (/j) contain a one-time code that the website never stores.

11. Children

PawTails is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child has used PawTails, contact us and we will delete the data.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version here with a new date. For important changes, we will also tell you in the app.
