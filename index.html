Questions

Something else? Write to us from Support.

Is PawTails for walks or for care?

Both. Walks show how much she sniffed and explored, and Today keeps track of feeding, medicine and the rest of her routine, for the whole family.

How does PawTails know she’s sniffing?

It looks at slow and stopped moments on your walk and counts them as sniff time. It’s an estimate, no collar or gadget needed.

Does it track my location all the time?

Routes are recorded only during a walk you start. If you turn on walk suggestions, PawTails also notices when you leave home. You can turn that off any time.

Can my family use it too?

Yes. Invite them with a QR code or a link, and you all share the same dog, care list and walks.

What if we skip a day?

Nothing breaks. There are no streaks, and every day starts fresh.
