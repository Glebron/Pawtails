Questions

Something else? Write to us from Support.

How does PawTails know she’s sniffing?

It looks at slow and stopped moments on your walk and counts them as sniff time. It’s an estimate, no collar or gadget needed.

Does it track my location all the time?

Routes are recorded only during a walk you start. If you turn on walk suggestions, PawTails also notices when you leave home.

Can my family use it too?

Yes. Invite them with a QR code or a link, and you all share the same dog, reminders and history.

What if we skip a day?

Nothing breaks. There are no streaks, and her rings start fresh every morning.
